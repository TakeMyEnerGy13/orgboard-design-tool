Frequently asked questions

How do I assign someone to a role?

Open a vacant role on the chart and choose Assign employee. Enter a name and save. To change who they report to, open the Role tab.

What do the card colors mean?

Green means targets are met. Red highlights a missed personal target; orange means someone on the team missed theirs. Gray means data is missing, and purple marks a vacancy. Open Results to see why.

How do I update a KPI?

In Metrics, select the actual value with the pencil, enter a new value and save. The chart updates for the employee and their managers. Output targets must be reached; cost limits must not be exceeded. Status compares values and does not measure lateness.

How do I ask AI about an employee?

In Results, choose Discuss with AI. Their name will appear in the composer. Ask about their performance or request a change. You can review the proposal before applying it.

What the colors mean

The color in the corner tells you where to look.

For example, Ethan has delivered 12 of 20 videos. His card is red. Olivia met her own target, but her card is orange because Ethan reports to her.

Update a result

The target is the number to reach. The actual is what has been achieved so far.

In Metrics, select the number with the pencil, update the actual and save. Open the card to review the plan. Additional objectives are listed under Goals.
